### C3. PCT Specification–Implementation 静态差异（可执行案例，第三轮削弱后）

**证据**：`PCT官方代码核查报告.md` §3.3（third-round-correction 版）+ 官方仓库 clone。

**可写 claim（静态数学事实，第三轮终审后仅剩一条）**：
- 论文声称标准 Jensen–Shannon 散度 `½[KL(p‖m)+KL(q‖m)]`；
- 官方代码 `train_clean.py` 用 `F.kl_div(p.log(), q)`，方向与标准 JS 不同，
  更准确称 **reverse-JS-type divergence**。
- **不再声称** joint 密度的任何差异：`K^⊤ K` 是配对样本 2D product-kernel KDE，
  与 standard 2D KDE **数学等价**（第三轮终审证明，我方此前"independence 假设"
  判断错误，已撤回）。

**明确不写**：
- ❌ 不写"outer-product / independence 假设 / 破坏物理依赖"（已撤回）
- ❌ 不写"官方实现导致 RMSE 差 3-8 倍"（loss 尺度混淆 + 复现 bug 已多次作废）
- ❌ 不写 gradient-matched 具体数字（需独立验证）

> 说明：C3 因此**削弱为单个 reverse-JS 候选差异**，不足以独立支撑一区级审计；
> 其角色降为"一个可执行的静态代码核查案例"，与 C1/C2 共同构成审计骨架。

## 一页 Abstract 草案

> Physics-constrained wind power forecasting assumes that embedding domain
> knowledge (power curves, Betz limits, wake models) improves or regularizes
> learned predictors. This paper audits that assumption at three levels that do
> not depend on any single trained model: (1) a lifecycle taxonomy distinguishing
> constraint provenance, application stage, inference-time requirement,
> implementation fidelity, and harm reporting; (2) a dual-annotator systematic
> coding of 21 recent physics-constrained wind forecasting papers (2023–2026),
> finding that 20/21 provide no public code (and the single exception, PCT,
> mismatches its paper's stated JS formulation), and that 18/21 report no
> negative effects of their constraints; (3) a static specification–implementation
> audit of the one available codebase, PCT, whose released implementation uses
> F.kl_div(log p, q) = KL(q‖p), i.e. a reverse-JS-type divergence rather than the
> paper's symmetric Jensen–Shannon divergence. Together these show that constraint
> provenance, fidelity, and harm are systematically unexamined in this field —
> not because constraints are necessarily harmful, but because their benefit is
> assumed rather than verified.
