# Mainline

Use this reference when drafting project plans, manuscript outlines, or reviewer responses around the mechanism hypothesis.

## Executable Hypothesis

Physical/engineering source-domain pretraining learns temporal stress descriptors that may transfer to financial drawdown-stress monitoring when the target market's regime structure, label prevalence, HGB anchor weakness, and time-series geometry align with the source-induced representation.

Do not treat this as proven. It is the organizing hypothesis that drives experiments.

## Narrative Shape

The strongest future paper should not say "source pretraining broadly works." It should say:

1. CSI300 internal audits show a real auxiliary rank signal.
2. Locked transfer to CSI500/SSE50 fails or weakens, proving the mechanism is conditional.
3. Negative controls show the signal is not label leakage, random rank smoothing, or arbitrary source shape.
4. Interventions identify which temporal stress descriptors are being moved.
5. Market-adapted validation and failure maps explain when the mechanism transfers or fails.

## Preferred Title Logic

Use titles like:

- "When Physical Time-Series Pretraining Transfers to Financial Drawdown-Stress Monitoring"
- "Market-Conditioned Transfer of Engineering Time-Series Representations for Financial Stress Ranking"
- "Auditing the Boundary of Cross-Domain Temporal Pretraining in Financial Drawdown Review"

Avoid titles implying universal transfer unless L4 evidence exists.

## Manuscript Spine

1. Problem: financial drawdown-stress labels are scarce and regime-dependent.
2. Hypothesis: physical/engineering temporal sources encode stress descriptors that can act as low-label auxiliary signals.
3. Internal evidence: strict CSI300 HGB-anchored rank fusion.
4. Boundary evidence: CSI500/SSE50 locked transfer non-confirmation.
5. Mechanism evidence: controls and interventions.
6. Conditional rule: when market adaptation or regime alignment is required.
7. Reproducibility: locked protocols, predictions, source data, and one-command artifacts.
