# Venue Gates

This reference stores the detailed gate logic for `aaai-eswa-skill`.

## ESWA

ESWA accepts a narrow but well-audited applied AI contribution if the claim is honest:

- CSI300 q=0.45 relative-downside review ranking.
- HGB-anchored rank fusion.
- Low-capacity auxiliary probe.
- Rank movement audit trail.
- Negative and boundary evidence reported in the main paper.
- No claim of general transfer or tail-risk warning.

## AAAI

AAAI requires a method contribution with generality and controls:

- Auxiliary representation admission framework.
- Validation-only gate.
- No-harm abstention/down-weighting.
- Negative controls for source validity.
- Cross-market improvement or correct rejection.
- Mechanism perturbations.
- Ranking and modern time-series baselines.
- Reproducibility package suitable for review.

## Claim Downgrade Rules

- L1 fails: write only rank-fusion interface, not source pretraining value.
- L2 fails without gate: do not submit AAAI as transfer/generalization paper.
- L3 fails: do not use mechanism language.
- Reproducibility incomplete: do not claim AAAI-ready.
