## Routing

- Research question clarification, deep research, literature review, systematic review, PRISMA, meta-analysis, fact checking:
  read `deep-research`, `literature-review`, or `research-lookup`.

- Research idea landing, full paper workflow, project planning, research-to-publication pipeline:
  read `academic-pipeline`; use `hypothesis-generation` for early hypothesis design.

- Manuscript drafting, Abstract/Introduction/Related Work/Method/Experiments/Conclusion, ML/CV/NLP paper writing, paragraph flow, reviewer-facing presentation:
  read `research-paper-writing`, `scientific-writing`, or `academic-paper`.

- Lowering AI feel, report tone, template tone, improving LaTeX prose rhythm:
  read `latex-rhythm-refiner`; preserve meaning and claims, not just synonyms.

- LaTeX paper from scratch, arXiv style, empirical paper structure, result backfill:
  read `paper-from-zero`, `arxiv-paper-writer`, `empirical-paper-writer`, or `results-backfill`.

- Claim-support analysis, reviewer risk, peer review simulation, formal reviews, re-review:
  read `academic-paper-reviewer`, `peer-review`, `scientific-critical-thinking`, or `scholar-evaluation`.

- Citation validation, DOI metadata, BibTeX generation, citation format and reference accuracy:
  read `citation-management`.

- Zotero local library search or PapersGPT for Zotero:
  read `papersgpt-for-zotero`. The downloaded Zotero plugin is at `E:\论文1\90_环境与第三方依赖\github-skills\papersgpt-for-zotero\papersgpt-v0.5.3.xpi` when that drive is available.

  read `venue-templates`, `research-grants`, `scientific-slides`, `scientific-schematics`, or `paper-2-web`.

## Operating Rules

- Default to one route at a time unless the user explicitly asks for a full pipeline.
- Do not treat summary as analysis: for paper analysis, inspect claims, evidence, assumptions, methods, limitations, and reviewer objections.
- Do not treat surface synonym replacement as lowering AI tone: revise rhythm, specificity, transitions, evidence placement, and authorial stance.
- For factual literature claims or current citation metadata, verify with reliable sources before asserting.
- For user manuscripts, keep edits scoped to the requested section unless the user asks for broader restructuring.
