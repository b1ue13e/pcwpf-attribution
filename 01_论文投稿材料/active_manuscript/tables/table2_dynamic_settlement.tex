\item \textbf{Notes}: (1) Values report multi-seed mean $\pm$ seed standard deviation across clean operational test periods (Kelmarsh: 4 seeds [42, 43, 44, 45]; Hill of Towie and Penmanshiel: 3 seeds [42, 43, 44]). Penalty schedules ($\lambda \in \{0.01, 0.1, 1.0\}$) were defined across a predefined sensitivity and stress-testing grid; model checkpoints and calibration offsets were selected exclusively on the 2016 validation/calibration partition, with the 2017 test year accessed only after protocol freezing. (2) $\text{Cost}_{\text{static}} = 3 \times \text{PinballLoss}(q=1/3)$ under post-hoc quantile calibration ($r=2$). (3) Pseudo-Scrambled arms permute the empirical wind-to-power mapping while preserving identical loss formulations and hyperparameters $\lambda$. At weak penalty ($\lambda=0.01$), genuine curve and scrambled curve yield near-identical loss ($0.08810$ vs.\ $0.08801$ on Kelmarsh; $0.08922$ vs.\ $0.08919$ on Hill of Towie). At strong penalty ($\lambda=1.0$) on Penmanshiel, genuine curve reduces loss ($-1.22\%$) whereas scrambled prior inflates loss ($+68.1\%$), isolating prior specificity. (4) Stale Training Proxy evaluates sensitivity to historical wind speed proxy lag $k \in \{6, 12, 18, 36\}$ (1h to 6h) in the training objective; the model takes only historical power at inference.
