\section{Related Work and Evidence-Gap Literature Audit}
\label{sec:related_work}

To contextualize why existing literature leaves the attribution question unresolved, we examine three intersecting research domains: (i) physics-constrained formulations in renewable energy forecasting, (ii) operational decision evaluation in electricity markets, and (iii) reproducibility and auditing standards in scientific machine learning. We then present a concise evidence-gap scoping audit across 46 contemporary studies, demonstrating that publications in the surveyed scoping cohort systematically omitted the negative controls and boundary stress tests required to establish domain-specific attribution.

\subsection{Physical Constraints in Wind Power Forecasting}
Embedding physical domain knowledge into deep neural networks aims to combine governing physical principles with data-driven function approximation \cite{karniadakis2021physics,raissi2019physics}. In wind power forecasting, physical constraints typically follow three main formulations:

\paragraph{Operational Envelope Bounds} Enforcing aerodynamic operating thresholds (such as cut-in, rated, and cut-out wind speeds, or Betz envelope limits) via bounded activations or post-hoc clipping guarantees that outputs respect nameplate boundaries \cite{tgdpf2025}. However, hard saturation can alter gradient flows near boundary cutoffs. In turbulent boundary-layer conditions where turbine generation hovers near rated capacity, zero-gradient saturation regions can impede gradient descent, restricting local adaptation to rapid meteorological transitions.

\paragraph{Kinematic Wake Formulations} Spatio-temporal forecasters commonly embed steady-state kinematic wake models---such as the 1D Jensen or Bastankhah--Port\'{e}-Agel Gaussian deficit equations \cite{jensen1983note,bastankhah2014new,s34_prx2023}---into Graph Neural Network (GNN) adjacency matrices. While this incorporates spatial layout geometry, replacing dynamic Navier--Stokes fluid mechanics with static engineering equations can restrict network flexibility under complex atmospheric stratification. Furthermore, published studies frequently attribute empirical gains to the embedded wake model without isolating whether the expressive capacity of the GNN backbone alone accounts for the improvement.

\paragraph{Distributional Regularization} Penalizing statistical divergence between predicted outputs and historical joint wind speed--power distributions (via Wasserstein distance or Jensen--Shannon divergence \cite{pct2026,tgdpf2025}) introduces complex optimization dynamics \cite{krishnapriyan2021}. Static distribution alignment regularizes predictions toward pre-computed empirical manifolds. When meteorological regimes shift or input observations suffer from temporal latency, divergence penalties can penalize genuine operational transients, creating optimization trade-offs between distribution fidelity and immediate dispatch accuracy.

\subsection{Decision-Theoretic Evaluation and Settlement Mechanics}
Wind power forecasting models have traditionally been evaluated using symmetric statistical error metrics, such as MAE, RMSE, and $R^2$ \cite{madsen2005,zhang2014wind}. Madsen et al. \cite{madsen2005} established standardized verification protocols highlighting the importance of evaluating models across horizons, operational regimes, and normalized capacity metrics.

However, power system operations demonstrate that statistical error reductions do not guarantee economic value under asymmetric market penalties \cite{weron2014,zhang2014wind}. Pinson et al. \cite{pinson2007trading} demonstrated that optimal bidding under asymmetric imbalance penalties is governed by conditional quantiles matching the operational cost ratio, highlighting the structural decoupling between point prediction accuracy and decision risk. Downstream grid dispatch penalizes reserve shortfalls far more severely than excess generation \cite{morales2014,yan2025economicvalue}. Furthermore, real-world balancing arrangements---such as the UK Elexon Balancing and Settlement Code (BSC)---aggregate energy deviations over discrete 30-minute settlement periods. Under isolated single-component accounting, unhedged cashflows can create apparent monetary surpluses through systematic under-commitment, decoupling cashflow from forecasting skill. Evaluating forecasters strictly on uncalibrated point errors or unhedged cashflows obscures their true operational efficacy.

\subsection{Reproducibility Standards and Software Auditing in Scientific AI}
Empirical machine learning has faced increasing scrutiny over data leakage, underspecification, and implementation discrepancies \cite{damour2022,gundersen2018,kapoor2023,lipton2019}. Kapoor and Narayanan \cite{kapoor2023} documented how subtle data leakage vectors undermine reproducibility across seventeen scientific domains. Gundersen and Kjensmo \cite{gundersen2018} evaluated 400 papers from major AI conferences, finding that fewer than 20\% shared executable source code or reproducible experimental protocols.

To address these vulnerabilities, procedural frameworks have gained traction. Pineau et al. \cite{pineau2021} developed the NeurIPS Machine Learning Reproducibility Checklist, demonstrating that explicit reporting mandates improve disclosure of code, data, hyperparameter choices, and error bounds. D'Amour et al. \cite{damour2022} showed that models with equivalent in-distribution test accuracy often diverge under distribution shift due to underspecified training criteria. Concurrently, Lipton and Steinhardt \cite{lipton2019} analyzed recurring pitfalls in empirical scholarship: confusing empirical association with theoretical explanation, failing to isolate independent sources of performance gains, and employing decorative mathematics that does not govern empirical outcomes.

\subsection{Evidence-Gap Scoping Audit: Why Current Evidence Cannot Settle Attribution}
\label{subsec:evidence_gap_audit}
While general machine learning venues have increasingly adopted rigorous reporting norms, physics-constrained wind forecasting has developed largely without standardized negative controls. To systematically examine current reporting patterns across the field, we conducted a scoping audit of $N=46$ contemporary studies (2023--2026) following PRISMA-ScR guidelines (full retrieval protocols, eligibility criteria, and multi-rater reliability tests are detailed in \ref{sec:appendix_audit_protocol} and \ref{sec:appendix_reliability}).

The scoping audit uncovers three systemic evaluation gaps:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
\item \textbf{Absence of Operational Failure Reporting (0/46 studies, 0.0\%)}: Across the 46 audited studies, not a single publication documented an operating regime, data budget, or stress-test condition in which physical constraints degraded downstream performance (95\% Wilson CI: [0.0\%, 7.7\%]). Only three studies (6.5\%) examined sensitivity to prior parameter variations; the remaining 43 tested constraints exclusively under nominal design configurations. Studies in the scoping cohort reported exclusively within nominal operating envelopes, leaving behavior outside nominal design conditions unexamined in the surveyed literature.
\item \textbf{Severe Open-Code Deficit (44/46 studies, 95.7\%)}: Only two of the 46 studies provided executable source code or reproducible computational scripts (95\% Wilson CI: [85.5\%, 98.8\%]). The remaining 44 studies omitted public repositories, preventing independent verification of mathematical loss implementations and computational graph executions.
\item \textbf{Confounded Module-Level Ablations (23/46 studies, 50.0\%)}: Exactly half of the audited studies evaluated physical constraints by excising entire multi-parameter neural blocks (e.g., stripping GNN layers or dual-stream attention branches) rather than toggling the constraint penalty multiplier ($\lambda=0$ vs.\ $\lambda>0$) under matched network capacity (95\% Wilson CI: [36.1\%, 63.9\%]). Because excising submodules alters parameter counts by thousands of weights, these ablations conflate representation capacity with physical inductive guidance.
\end{enumerate}

As summarized in Table~\ref{tab:lit_comparison}, existing literature surveys have focused predominantly on model taxonomies without auditing lifecycle provenance, software fidelity, or ablation confounding. These three structural gaps demonstrate why conventional literature comparisons cannot answer the central attribution question: without form-matched negative controls, disclosed data budgets, and unconfounded parameter ablations, observed empirical gains cannot be reliably attributed to valid physical domain knowledge.
