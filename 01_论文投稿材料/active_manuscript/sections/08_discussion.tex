\section{Discussion, Boundary Contracts, and Compliance Protocol}
\label{sec:discussion}

\subsection{Four Empirical Boundary Conditions for Physics-Constrained Models}
The scoping audit, algebraic code examination, and prospective ablations converge on four boundary conditions that determine when physical priors help, when they are neutral, and when they harm.

\paragraph{Sample Restriction and Prior Misspecification Vulnerability}
On Penmanshiel ($N_{\text{train}}=1,583$), the calibrated power curve prior at $\lambda=1.0$ reduces asymmetric dispatch cost from $0.09881 \pm 0.00027$ to $0.09760 \pm 0.00044$ ($-1.22\%$). A form- and weight-matched scrambled pseudo-prior raises dispatch cost to $0.16611 \pm 0.00335$ ($+68.1\%$). The divergence between these outcomes---authentic priors securing modest downside protection whereas corrupted priors inflict severe damage---demonstrates a fundamental risk-return asymmetry: under strong regularization, whatever information the prior encodes is amplified, including its misspecifications. Cross-site comparisons cannot isolate this effect because they confound site topography with sample volume. In the data-rich regime (Kelmarsh, $N_{\text{train}}=5,098$), unconstrained DLinear ($0.08812$), genuine physics ($0.08791$), and scrambled prior ($0.08801$) are numerically clustered, indicating hypothesis-space saturation.

\paragraph{Low-Penalty Scale and Non-Specific Numerical Regularization}
At $\lambda = 0.01$ on Kelmarsh, a genuine physical prior ($0.08810 \pm 0.00009$) and a structurally scrambled pseudo-prior ($0.08801 \pm 0.00008$) yield closely matched decision costs with overlapping seed margins. When random power curve permutations reproduce the same outcome as domain aerodynamics, the penalty term behaves empirically as a non-specific regularizer / numerical smoothing term rather than encoding domain physical laws. Published claims of domain physical benefit under $\lambda \in [10^{-4}, 10^{-2}]$ require negative-control ablations and sensitivity testing at $\lambda \ge 0.1$ to be credible.

\paragraph{Monotonic Cost Inflation under Training Proxy Observation Staleness}
When physical constraint formulations rely on meteorological proxies to query empirical power curves during loss computation, practitioners may be tempted to substitute lagged historical observations when contemporaneous or forward weather forecasts are unavailable. Our lag scan ($k \in \{6, 12, 18, 36\}$ steps, 1\,h to 6\,h) demonstrates strictly monotonic cost inflation across all three wind farms. On Hill of Towie, dispatch risk climbs from $0.09022$ at $k=6$ to $0.09257$ at $k=36$, exceeding the unconstrained baseline ($0.08924$) at $k \approx 12$. We emphasize that this experiment characterizes the sensitivity of the training objective to temporal misalignment between the forecast horizon and the historical meteorological proxy, rather than an inference-time telemetry delay (the forecasting backbone takes only past power measurements during inference). Nonetheless, it demonstrates that offline regularization against lagged weather proxies rapidly erodes inductive guidance, presenting an operational limitation for proxy-dependent constraint designs.

\paragraph{Metric Decoupling and the Zero-Bid Diagnostic}
In stylized wholesale power accounting coupling forward commitments with balancing cash-out settlement \cite{pinson2007trading}, total commercial revenue is $R_{\text{total}} = P_{\text{F}} \cdot q + P_{\text{imb}} \cdot (y - q)$, where $q$ denotes the forward-contracted position in the stylized model. Evaluations that drop the forward commitment term and report only balancing cashflow are measuring imbalance rent, not forecast skill. As an analytical imbalance-settlement and decision-risk diagnostic, the degenerate Zero-Bid baseline ($q \equiv 0$) provides a mathematical \textit{reductio ad absurdum}: it extracts £223k--£243k per turbine-year across all three farms without generating forecasts, simply by converting all physical generation into uncontracted spill rewarded at $P_{\text{imb}}$. This confirms that standalone unhedged balancing cashflows are decoupled from forecasting skill, and that assessing full commercial value requires joint modeling of forward contract revenue ($P_{\text{F}} \cdot q$) and imbalance settlement. Operational evaluations must report asymmetric Pinball loss at the market penalty ratio or coupled forward-plus-imbalance cashflows.

\subsection{Attributional Identification: What Is Identified vs.\ What Is Not}
\label{sec:identification}
To maintain rigorous scientific standards and prevent inductive over-reach, we explicitly articulate the attributional identification boundaries of this audit:

\paragraph{What Is Formally Identified}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*, label=(\alph*)]
\item \textbf{Data-Starvation and Information-Budget Dynamics}: In subsampled training sequences holding site topography and turbine hardware constant (Figure~\ref{fig:within_site_learning_curve}, Table~\ref{tab:information_budget}), an advantage is observed under restricted model-training windows ($N_{\text{train}} \le 1{,}000$). By evaluating a strictly information-budget matched $2 \times 2$ ablation at $N_{\text{train}}=500$, we isolate that authentic physical inductive guidance accounts for the majority of downside risk reduction ($-1.54\%$ on Kelmarsh, $-3.34\%$ on Hill of Towie) even when restricted to the identical 21-day data window, while the auxiliary historical seasonal pool provides an additional incremental gain ($-0.46\%$ and $-0.28\%$).
\item \textbf{Physical Specificity vs.\ Non-Specific Smoothing}: By comparing genuine power curves against form- and weight-matched pseudo-scrambled controls under identical network architectures, optimization schedules, and loss parameters, we isolate true aerodynamic guidance from general numerical regularization.
\item \textbf{Loss Sensitivity to Historical Proxy Latency}: By scanning proxy staleness $k \in \{6, \dots, 36\}$ steps within the training loss, we isolate the mathematical degradation induced by lagged meteorological inputs when forward weather forecasts are absent.
\item \textbf{Software Discrepancy in Divergence Optimization}: Static AST analysis and gradient derivation isolate an exact algebraic inversion in the PCT code repository, establishing a concrete implementation divergence between published formulas and software execution.
\item \textbf{Metric Decoupling of Imbalance Cashflows}: The degenerate Zero-Bid baseline mathematically separates standalone unhedged balancing cashflows from true predictive accuracy.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{What Is Not Identified (Confounding Disclaimers)}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*, label=(\roman*)]
\item \textbf{Cross-Site Topographical Causation}: Differences across Kelmarsh, Hill of Towie, and Penmanshiel confound topographic complexity with turbine ratings (2.05\,MW vs.\ 2.30\,MW), hub heights, wake geometry, and local micro-climates. We do not claim that complex terrain causally mandates physical constraints.
\item \textbf{Global Generalization Beyond Scoped Cohort}: The 95.7\% code deficit and 50.0\% confounded ablation rates characterize the audited 46-study scoping cohort (2023--2026) under PRISMA-ScR protocols, not the entire machine learning or power engineering literature.
\item \textbf{Software Bugs as Universal Practice}: Auditing PCT and PINN-UQ demonstrates that code-manuscript discrepancies can occur; it does not imply that all published physics-informed energy forecasting codebases contain errors.
\item \textbf{Decoupled Calibration Universality}: Counterexamples 1 and 2 are formal existence / possibility proofs establishing the theoretical limits of scalar quantile offsets under spatial heteroscedasticity, not an empirical assertion that scalar calibration always underperforms joint asymmetric training.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{External Validity Boundaries}
\label{sec:boundary_contract}
Five operational boundaries define the validity envelope of our empirical findings:

\paragraph{Boundary 1: Sample Volume and Hypothesis-Space Saturation}
Within-site learning curves confirm that physical constraint benefits concentrate under data-scarce regimes in our tested configurations ($N_{\text{train}} \le 1,000$, Figure~\ref{fig:within_site_learning_curve}). As training data expands to $N_{\text{train}} = 5{,}000$, hypothesis-space saturation reduces the gap between genuine-prior and unconstrained models to within 0.33\% on Kelmarsh, while scrambled priors remain severely detrimental throughout. Continuous corruption tests further establish a narrow fault-tolerance window ($\alpha^* \approx 0.07$ on Penmanshiel, Figure~\ref{fig:corruption_gradient}). Beyond this threshold, engineering drift exceeding 10\% aerodynamic capacity loss or $\pm 5\%$ anemometer bias flips the nominal physical prior from an asset into an operational liability.

\paragraph{Boundary 2: Proxy Misalignment and Observation Staleness}
For proxy-dependent physical constraints, regularization effectiveness depends on temporal alignment between meteorological proxies and target horizons ($k \le 6$ steps). Beyond this, monotonic cost inflation is observed on all three farms ($+2.6\%$ on Hill of Towie at $k=36$). Offline training objectives that substitute stale historical proxies for contemporaneous weather data induce substantial performance degradation.

\paragraph{Boundary 3: Market Settlement Diagnostic Envelope}
Economic findings serve as an imbalance-settlement and decision-risk diagnostic bounded by wholesale arrangements coupling forward contracting with asymmetric single-price balancing cashouts ($r \ge 1.0$) and half-hour aggregation of modelled imbalance exposures under UK BSC cash-out prices. Standalone imbalance cashflows do not represent full commercial value and do not generalize to symmetric single-settlement systems or flat feed-in tariffs.

\paragraph{Boundary 4: Computational Graph Audit and Implementation Parity}
Software conclusions apply to PCT commit \texttt{65941ec} and the computational graphs audited here. Unreleased proprietary codebases require independent static graph audit before implementation fidelity claims can be admitted.

\paragraph{Boundary 5: Literature Scoping Scope and Architectural Probe Envelopes}
The systematic literature audit is strictly bounded to the 46 studies identified under our PRISMA-ScR protocol; the documented 95.7\% code deficit and 50.0\% confounded ablation prevalence characterize reporting practices within this scoping cohort rather than an exhaustive census of all hybrid machine learning. Similarly, our PatchTST evaluations function as an architectural sensitivity probe under fixed nominal regularization ($\lambda=0.01$); while demonstrating that qualitative downside risk reduction persists across non-linear deep representations, joint cross-backbone $\lambda$ grid searches remain an open direction for future benchmarking.

\subsection{Two Conjectures Falsified During the Audit}
Two initial hypotheses were refuted during the empirical audit. First, forensic tensor derivation showed that PCT's product-kernel KDE preserves bivariate coupling algebraically, refuting the conjecture of marginal independence. Second, market diagnostic probing showed that post-calibration cashflow inversions are governed by settlement price asymmetries rather than physical regularizers. Documenting these falsification outcomes provides a transparent account of our investigative trajectory and prevents post-hoc rationalization.

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Gate M1 (Parameter-Isolated Ablations):} Parameter-matched comparisons ($\lambda=0$ vs.\ $\lambda>0$) should be required to isolate physical prior contributions, avoiding submodule excisions (e.g., stripping GNN layers) that conflate representational capacity with domain knowledge.
\item \textbf{Gate M2 (Pseudo-Scrambled Negative Controls):} A minimum credible evaluation of physical reasoning claims should include form- and weight-matched permutation controls to separate aerodynamic guidance from non-specific numerical smoothing.
\item \textbf{Gate M3 (Operational Proxy Staleness Stress-Tests):} Multi-step constraint evaluations should avoid assuming zero-latency future weather inputs, reporting performance across $k \in \{6, \dots, 36\}$ steps when forward NWP feeds are absent.
\item \textbf{Gate M4 (Coupled Forward and Imbalance Evaluation):} Evaluations should avoid standalone unhedged balancing cashflows, reporting instead asymmetric Pinball loss at the market penalty ratio or coupled forward-plus-imbalance P\&L ($P_{\text{F}} \cdot q + P_{\text{imb}} \cdot (y - q)$).
\item \textbf{Gate M5 (Static Computational Graph Audit):} Reviewers should expect executable training scripts or verified computational graphs, verifying that software loss execution matches published definitions in argument ordering and reduction semantics.
